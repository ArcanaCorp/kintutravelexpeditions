## This Is Where Nature, Culture and Adventure Meet

It’s time to slow down, reconnect, and experience Peru beyond the obvious. In Jauja and the Mantaro Valley, nature is sacred, culture is alive, and every journey becomes a story worth telling. From breathtaking canyons and highland lagoons to ancient archaeological sites and living Andean traditions, this is where meaningful travel begins.  

Whether you’re seeking adrenaline-filled adventures through canyons, waterfalls and stone forests, or immersive cultural experiences surrounded by history, wellness and ancestral rituals, you’re exactly where you need to be. With full-day experiences, authentic local gastronomy, pristine landscapes and routes that connect the Andes with the high jungle, Kintu Travel Expeditions invites you to explore Peru in a deeper, more conscious way.
